# CV-Aligned Executive Website Cleanup

## Removed from the public website

- Unsupported `320K+ Validations` claim.
- R&D Lab and speculative innovation content.
- Standalone Release Engineering & DevEx case study.
- Hobbies & Activities, including politics, podcasts, and reading.
- Subjective circular ratings such as client satisfaction and innovation adoption.
- Skill progress bars and percentage-style proficiency visualization.
- Ambiguous organization logos: Plan 2, ESCD, BGICC, and Arab Organizers.

## Consolidated

- Executive Technology Operating Model reduced to four CV-supported pillars:
  - Strategy
  - Architecture
  - Governance
  - Delivery
- Skills reduced to selected executive and technical capabilities.
- “My Strengths” replaced with “Executive Capabilities.”
- Mentoring and knowledge sharing retained as a professional contribution.

## Portfolio treatment

Twelve earlier websites and digital products were moved to a clearly labeled historical archive in `portfolio.html`. They remain available as delivery evidence but are separated from the executive platform portfolio.
