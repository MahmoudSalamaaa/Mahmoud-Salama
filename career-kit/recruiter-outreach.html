Recommended outreach sequence

Before applying

Identify a recruiter, employee, or hiring manager. Ask for insight or a referral using a short, relevant message.

After applying

Send a brief message referencing two requirements and the application date.

Follow-up

Follow up after 7-10 days, then re-engage recruiters after 3-4 weeks with a meaningful update.
