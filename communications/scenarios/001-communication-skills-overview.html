INFORMATION GIVEN You will talk to parent or adolescent about new diagnosis of chronic condition or change in the treatment because of deterioration.

STEPS:

I R C AP

  • I: INTRODUCEYOURSELF ✓ R: RAPPORT
  • C: CLARIFYYOURROLE
  • A:ASKABOUTATTENDENCE
  • P: CHECKPRIORKNOWLEGE

Example

I amhere today to talk to you about ... health, but before westart are you waiting for someone to attend with us, or shall westart?

Tell mefirst, what's your update regarding ... health?

I came today to talk to you about the results of tests done for him which revealed that he is diagnosed to have ... disease, did you hear about it before?

Thenyou will start to explain for himin simple words what is this disease with simple drawing & the decision taken by the team regarding the managementplan for the child

They mayagree or disagree to the newplan of management.

Ask them why?Explore their concern, try to convince them.

If you talk about treatment, describe the newtreatment, how it works, the route of its administration, for howlong he will take it.

Talk about the risks and benefits of the treatment strategy.

If still not convinced explain the side effects of not taking the medications or following the management plans.

If still, you can offer to arrange a meeting with the consultant or the team responsible for following up the child.

Finish the scenario with the summary and provide leaflets and trusted websites to read more about the disease.

Examples for information given scenarios:

1. Patient newly diagnosed DMand the team decided to start insulin.

2. Patient newly diagnosed NF1.

3. Patient with asthma and team decided to start steroid inhaler.

4. Turner $ newly diagnosed.

BREAKINGBADNEWS

− Same introduction IRCAP, important to ask about attender then starts to deliver the new information in a low tone voice, give the role player the space to replay and express her feelings and ask questions.

− Clarify all information clearly and honestly with all suspected complications.

− Important to highlight the systems affected, in the form of what, investigations if needed for follow up the child, team he will follow with, is it curable or not.

− Arrange another meeting if they will have any concern. Provide written leaflets and support group (if chronic condition).

CONSENT

Sameintroduction IRCAP,talking briefly about the child condition that requires to doa procedure, that's whyyou will talk the parent about the decision taken by our team to do this procedure for the child. For any procedure: draw simple drawing for good explanation.

− Before the procedure: preparation regarding being fasting for 6_ 8 hours, he will be on IV fluids, sleeping medicine will be given or numbness cream according to the procedure.

− During the procedure: explain howit will be done, assuring them that the team will perform it are highly qualified, they did it manytimes, following strict hygienic measures.

− After the procedure: we will monitor him for any complications (bleeding, infection, anaphylaxis to anaesthesia), whenhe will be discharged.

Examples for consent scenarios:

1. Leukaemia for B.Maspiration consent.

2. Consent for fundoplication.

3. Consent for gastrostomy.

4. Consent for L.P in meningitis.

CRITICALINCIDENTS

You did a mistake to a child, and you will apologize to the parents for this error, you must reassure the parents that their child is stable, we are doing investigations to know the error how happened, critical incident form done, practice manager had been informed, talk about the medicine given and its side effects with all precautions to manage it. What will you do to be sure it not happens again by making double check for his file and put florescence marker.

ETHICS

− Anethical dilemma is a problem in the decision-making process between two possible options, neither of which is acceptable from an ethical perspective. Although we face many ethical and moral problems in our lives, most of them come with relatively straightforward solutions.

Examples for ethical dilemma

− DNR,do not resuscitate a baby 23 wwith brainstem death.

− HIVdisclosure.

− HLHS,hypoplastic left heart syndrome for disconnecting him from ventilator.

− Ethics of research on babies.

EDUCATIONTASK Usually, you will talk to a colleague, medical student or nurse about medical conditions in medical terms. Discuss the issue in detail for diagnosis, causes, and management. It must be a double wayconservation between you and the medical personnel.

Examples for education tasks

2. Talk to a medical student about functional constipation

3. Talk to medical student about exchange transfusion in babies with ABOincompatibility.

Neonatal Cooling

Yourtask:

MRCPCHCommunication Scenario: Station Title: Teaching a Junior Doctor – Neonatal Cooling (Therapeutic Hypothermia)

Scenario Stem (Candidate Instructions): You are a paediatric registrar in the neonatal unit.

Ajunior doctor has asked youto explain neonatal therapeutic cooling for babies with suspected hypoxic- ischemic encephalopathy (HIE).

  • Explain clearly and in a structured way:
  • Definition
  • Indications (baby selection criteria)
  • Whywe perform cooling
  • Methods of cooling
  • Monitoring and complications
  • Outcomes
  • Encourage interaction and check understanding
