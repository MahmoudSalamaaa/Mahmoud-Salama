Explain what HUS is & how it happened: During the gastroenteritis infection, toxins are released. These toxins damage the delicate lining of the very small vessels within the body. Once damaged, these vessels become swollen and blood cells passing through the vessels are destroyed. These damaged blood cells clump together and block small vessels in the kidney known as the glomeruli. This leads to problems with the kidneys’ ability to filter and eliminate waste products. The kidneys are unable to remove fluid and waste that they would normally do, resulting in kidney failure.

From where he got the infection: This bacterium is found in contaminated foods like meat, organic vegetables, ground meat, dairy products and juices. Lakes or water contaminated with faces is another source. Minced beef and dairy produce are the most implicated foods. Then transmitted from the contaminated food though the hand to the mouth( faeco oral).

He will be admitted? Right now, there is no need for admission. However, weneed to do some tests of the kidney, cbc, follow his renal function and body salts. If the results are normal there is no need for treatment or antibiotics. If the tests are positive, we need to involve a kidney doctor and blood doctor for admission. Also, he will need to adjust the fluids and salts he is taking and mayneed blood according to the result of the blood test.

We need to screen other family members & notify the public health authority (if not mentioned).
