Professional Summary

A committed and adaptable Medical Doctor with over 12 years of progressive clinical and humanitarian experience spanning pediatrics, emergency medicine, primary care, clinical nutrition, and medical coordination in both stable and crisis-affected settings. Demonstrates strong operational and clinical leadership through extensive work with Médecins Sans Frontières (MSF), supporting mission-wide medical strategy, emergency preparedness, and field operations across diverse cultural and resource-limited environments.
