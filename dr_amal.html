About Me

A dynamic public health leader dedicated to architecting transformative training programs and captivating community awareness campaigns. I thrive on translating complex data into evidence-based strategies, driving successful projects and forging powerful community alliances.

With over two decades of experience in public health and community medicine, I have consistently demonstrated excellence in leadership, strategic planning, and innovative program development. My expertise spans health technology assessment, economic evaluation, and community engagement, making me a trusted voice in Egypt's healthcare transformation journey.

My Mission
