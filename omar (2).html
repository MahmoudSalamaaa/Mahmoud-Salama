About Omar

Omar Mahmoud is a curious and energetic 10‑year‑old boy who attends Anglo American School in Cairo. He loves discovering new things about the world around him and beyond. Whether it’s learning about the planets, drawing colorful pictures, or building with Legos, Omar’s imagination never stops.

At school, he enjoys science and math, and he often dreams of becoming an astronaut or an engineer so he can build rockets that soar beyond the stars. His cheerful personality and love for adventure make every day an exciting journey!

Hobbies & Lifestyle

When Omar isn’t busy with schoolwork, you can find him playing soccer with his friends or zooming around the house pretending to be Sonic the Hedgehog. He loves drawing cartoon characters, reading comics, and solving puzzles.

Omar also enjoys spending time outdoors, exploring nature, and going on family trips. With a heart full of curiosity and a mind ready for adventure, there’s never a dull moment in Omar’s life!

Favorites

Favorite Book

Omar’s favorite book is “The Wild Robot” by Peter Brown. He loves how Roz the robot learns to survive in the wild and make friends with the animals on the island. The book sparks his imagination about friendly robots and adventures in nature.

Favorite Characters

He is a big fan of Sonic the Hedgehog and the Minions. Sonic’s speed and bravery inspire Omar to run fast and be heroic, while the silly antics of the Minions always make him laugh.

Favorite Quote

“To infinity and beyond!” – this famous line from Buzz Lightyear sums up Omar’s spirit of exploration and adventure.
