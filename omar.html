👦 About Me

Hi! I’m Omar Mahmoud, a 10-year-old explorer who loves discovering how things work. I spend a lot of time thinking about the future, building small ideas, and dreaming about new inventions that can make life easier for everyone. 🚀

Ever since I was little, I’ve been fascinated by stars, planets, and technology. I love to imagine flying through space in my own rocket, visiting other worlds, and bringing back cool alien technology to help people on Earth. 🌌

When I’m not studying or playing, I enjoy learning about computers, robots, and how apps are made. I believe that even kids can build amazing things with creativity and curiosity. 💡

My favorite time of the day is when I can code, draw, or design something new. I think of technology as magic — the kind of magic you can actually learn! That’s why I want to study computer science and become a great inventor someday. 🧑‍💻

My biggest dream is to start my own company called Asmarto — a place where I can build smart and helpful software that makes people’s lives better. Until then, I’ll keep learning, creating, and enjoying my journey among the stars. 🌠

🏫 My School

I study at the Anglo American School in Egypt, and it’s one of my favorite places in the world! Every day feels like a new adventure filled with fun, discovery, and learning. 🎒✨

Our teachers are amazing — they encourage us to ask questions, explore our ideas, and always believe in what we can do. I especially enjoy classes that let me be creative and use my imagination. 💭💡

My favorite subjects are Physical Education, Music, and Computer Studies. In P.E., I love playing football and running with my friends. In Music, I enjoy learning rhythms and songs that make everyone smile. And in Computer Studies, I get to discover how computers “think” — it’s like learning the language of the future! ⚽🎵💻

I also like participating in school projects and science fairs. I’ve helped create small coding projects, built simple games, and worked with my classmates to solve problems using teamwork and creativity. 🧠🤝

My school is more than just a building — it’s a place where I grow, learn new things every day, and build the confidence to follow my dreams. 🌍📚
