Use platforms strategically

For senior roles, prioritize official employer portals and LinkedIn networking. Treat auto-apply tools carefully, use precise filters, and verify the employer, domain, location eligibility and application route before sharing personal information. Freelance and hourly-work platforms are secondary channels, not substitutes for targeted executive applications.
