Role evidence

What the vacancy is really hiring for

Not a general IT manager. It is an enterprise systems owner working under the Head of Information Systems, based in Abidjan, with mobility across WaCA intervention zones.

Primary objective

Lead management and optimisation of enterprise IT systems across headquarters and missions, ensuring security, availability and performance while aligning local systems with MSF SITS global platforms.

Operating interfaces

Head of IS, Security team, Support team, shared/global technology services, functional resource owners, vendors, project teams and mission stakeholders.

AWS: evidence and safe conclusion

Strong WaCA signal

Your written exam

AWS questions in the selection exam make AWS highly relevant to preparation. They may reflect the current environment, an upcoming roadmap, vendor architecture, or a test of cloud-neutral competence.

Not public confirmation

WaCA vacancy

The public role description explicitly names Azure knowledge but does not publicly name AWS. Do not state that WaCA runs AWS as an established fact until the panel confirms it.

MSF-wide evidence

Public MSF material

An MSF evaluation of the REACH platform documented AWS security and cross-region backup recommendations. This confirms AWS exists in the wider MSF technology landscape, but it is not proof of WaCA's architecture.

Recommended wording: “AWS was included in my written assessment, so I have prepared it as an important part of the role. I also understand the vacancy names Azure. I would like to confirm how WaCA divides workloads, ownership and standards between AWS, Azure, SITS and local systems.”

Your fit and likely challenges

Strong fit

  • 18+ years in enterprise systems and digital transformation
  • Healthcare and public-sector scale
  • Secure API and ERP/CRM integration
  • IAM, RBAC, MFA/OTP, audit and security governance
  • Data platforms, reporting and stakeholder leadership
  • Business continuity, backup and DR planning

Prepare carefully

  • Demonstrable hands-on depth in AWS plus current Azure/M365/MDM administration
  • Direct NGO and humanitarian operating experience
  • Professional French requirement
  • Explaining a move from a very senior national title into this reporting line
  • Avoiding confidential or inflated claims when using national-scale examples

MSF WaCA strategic context

MSF's 2025 evaluation of the WaCA 2021–2025 strategy highlighted growth and responsiveness, but also gaps between ambition and implementation capacity, inconsistent internal alignment, resource constraints, and under-resourced data integration and monitoring. For an IS candidate, this implies that practical prioritisation, institutional strengthening, field feedback loops and sustainable systems may matter as much as technical sophistication.

Interview messages to repeat

Stabilise before transform

First know the portfolio, owners, risks and recovery readiness.

Shared standards, local reality

Align with SITS while documenting justified field exceptions.

Technology serves operations

Measure success by continuity, safety, adoption and medical/mission enablement.
