30–60–90 day plan

A credible first-quarter plan for a manager joining an existing systems function. The theme is discover → stabilise → govern and improve.

Days 1–30

Discover & align

  • Meet Head of IS, Systems, Support, Security, SITS contacts and business owners
  • Map systems, integrations, AWS/Azure accounts and subscriptions, hosting, identity, licences, contracts and costs
  • Review current incidents, known risks, audits and project commitments
  • Identify critical services, owners and existing RTO/RPO
  • Visit or interview representative missions
  • Agree decision rights, reporting and escalation cadence

Outputs

Stakeholder map, initial application register, top-risk list, listening report.

Days 31–60

Stabilise & prioritise

  • Validate AWS/Azure landing-zone controls, security baselines, privileged access, patching and MDM coverage
  • Review on-premises, AWS, Azure and SaaS backup/recovery evidence; schedule priority restore tests
  • Clarify RACI between Systems, Support, Security, SITS and vendors
  • Identify EOL systems, undocumented dependencies and single-person knowledge
  • Reconcile licences and vendor SLAs
  • Create prioritised backlog with value, risk, cost and dependencies

Outputs

Risk treatment plan, service RACI, quick wins, draft systems roadmap.

Days 61–90

Deliver & institutionalise

  • Deliver two or three visible high-value improvements
  • Approve portfolio, lifecycle and documentation standards
  • Launch systems scorecard and monthly service review
  • Agree integration governance and data-quality ownership
  • Establish vendor/licence review calendar
  • Present 12–18 month roadmap and capacity plan

Outputs

Measured quick wins, approved roadmap, KPI baseline, governance calendar.

Possible quick wins — only after validation

Licence cleanup

Recover unused licences and automate offboarding.

Recovery test

Prove restore capability for one critical service.

Admin hardening

Separate privileged identities and review MFA coverage.

System register

Create one authoritative view of owners, cloud location, risks and lifecycle.

Interview phrasing: “I would not arrive with a pre-selected technology programme. My first commitment would be to establish evidence, relationships and operational priorities, then deliver quick wins that support a credible roadmap.”
