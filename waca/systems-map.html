Questions to ask the interview panel

  • What are the top three enterprise-system risks or priorities for the next 12 months?
  • Which platforms are provided by SITS, and which are owned directly by WaCA?
  • Which workloads currently run on AWS versus Azure, and who owns their landing zones and security controls?
  • How are responsibilities divided between Systems, Support and Security?
  • What is the current maturity of the application inventory and lifecycle roadmap?
  • Which systems create the most incidents or user frustration today?
  • What would success after six months look like?
  • How much travel is expected, and which intervention zones are priorities?
  • How are French and English used in daily work and in the technical team?
Do not say: “WaCA definitely uses AWS because it was in the exam,” or “MSF uses UniField, DHIS2 and another tool everywhere.” Say: “The written exam makes AWS highly relevant, while public MSF evidence confirms AWS elsewhere; I would validate WaCA's actual cloud portfolio and service ownership.”
